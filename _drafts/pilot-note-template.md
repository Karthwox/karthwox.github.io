## The idea

What you expected and why. State the hypotheses explicitly (H1, H2, ...), and say what result would have made the idea worth pursuing.

## Pilot setup

- **Data / models:** where it came from, how much of it, and any filters you applied.
- **Measures:** how each concept was turned into a number.
- **Comparisons:** what was compared with what, the controls, and how intervals were computed.

## What I saw

One subsection per finding, each with the number, its interval and the sample size. Include at least one figure or table. Report the results that didn't work as well.

## Why I stopped

Which link in the argument failed, or what it would take to make the result convincing, and why that isn't worth doing right now.

## What would make it worth reopening

Specific conditions, data or designs that would change your mind.

## Lessons for anyone trying something similar

Methodological traps and shortcuts that apply beyond this one idea.
